Shop notes

  1. Print with scaling off. Measure the 50 mm and 1 in squares; if they are off, fix printer scaling before cutting.
  2. For multi-page prints, trim each page on its frame line along the dotted overlap edge, line up the grey crosses, and tape.
  3. Glue the template to the copper (spray adhesive or glue stick), scribe or cut on the solid line, and cut tabs before folding.
  4. The printed side is the outside of the cone. Roll along the dotted bend lines; on skewed cones they fan unevenly, so follow them rather than rolling evenly.
  5. Pattern is drawn on the mid-thickness of the metal, so the "measured to" setting decides whether your diameters land on the inside or outside.
  6. Lap the plain seam edge over the solder lap. Check the formed piece against the reference-line lengths and the angle marks on the edges.
  7. Tabs: the solder lap stays flat and hides behind the opposite seam edge. Base and top tabs fold on their dashed lines after the seam is soldered. Fold them outward for a visible flange or inward to hide them, then solder them to a disc. Hover the ? next to each setting for sizes.
